MATH 212 · Multivariable Calculus

Interactive lessons for MATH 212 (Multivariable Calculus, Stewart chapters 12 to 16, Fall 2026, Gwaltney). Each module teaches, lets you explore, then drills. Progress is saved in this browser.

Answer boxes work like WebAssign's calcPad: typing sqrt, /, ^, pi, or < > builds the math as you type. Use the right arrow key to step out of a radical, fraction, or exponent.

Midterm 1 material: vectors, curves, scalar line integrals, partial derivatives

Midterm 2 material: optimization and multiple integrals

Final material: vector calculus

Auxiliary: matrices

Not part of the course sequence. Built for CMOR 220, kept here because a little matrix fluency helps with cross products, Jacobians, and the Hessian test.

Module files live in the top folder. Open any module directly, or come back here with the "All modules" link in each module's sidebar.
