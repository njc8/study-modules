MATH 102 · Single Variable Calculus II

Interactive lessons for MATH 102 (Fall 2026, Xiang Yao), following the lecture order: techniques of integration, polar and parametric curves, improper integrals, sequences and series, Taylor series, and complex numbers. Stewart 9e, sections 5.5, 7.1 to 7.4, 7.8, 10.1 to 10.4, 11.1 to 11.11, and Appendix H.

Answer boxes work like WebAssign's calcPad: typing sqrt, /, ^, pi builds the math as you type; | gives absolute value; n! is a factorial; + C is optional on antiderivatives. Use the right arrow key to step out of a radical, fraction, or exponent.
